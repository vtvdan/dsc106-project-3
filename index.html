Polar Amplification: Latitudinal Asymmetry in Projected Surface Warming

The Arctic is warming much faster than the rest of the planet, a pattern called polar amplification. Pick an emissions scenario and move the year to see how much faster. Hover the chart to read any latitude band.

Each point on the line is a band of latitude (north is up). The farther right it sits, the more that band has warmed since 1995-2014. The straight dashed vertical line is the global average, and the shaded area is the gap from it.

Write-up

Motivation

We chose this topic because of the stark difference in warming in the arctic compared to the rest of the world we found in our exploratory visualizations. We also recognized the significant impact arctic warming will have, leading to a rising sea level and other environmental effects. A viewer of this visualization should see how warming plays out over time and in different scenarios and see the particularly large impact on the arctic.
